## What this shows

- **All 15 tasks are registered, isolated, and genuinely buggy.** Every
  seed scores below full reward. 13 of the 14 agent-facing tasks score
  0.5 (tests pass, hidden behavior check fails); `pricing_discount_rounding`
  scores **0.0**, confirmed against its own design intent
  (TASK_SUITE_DESIGN.md / CHANGELOG "Phase 5"): its copied-in visible test
  suite already fails on the seed, so the agent must diagnose a visible
  failure rather than discover a hidden one.
- **The 0.0/0.5/0.85/1.0 reward scale is uniform across all 14 agent-facing
  tasks**, not just `bugfix_inventory`. A correct fix with no regression
  test scores exactly 0.85 on every one of them - the same number the real
  `manual-baseline-01` episode scored - which is real evidence the scale
  means the same thing on every task, not just an assumption stated in
  TASK_SUITE_DESIGN.md §11.
- **The anti-reward-hacking mutation check generalizes to all 14
  agent-facing tasks, not just the 2 already documented in RESEARCH.md.**
  A vacuous regression test (`assert True`) is correctly rejected (scored
  0.85, not 1.0) on every one of them. RESEARCH.md's own flagship
  experiment and its replication demonstrate this mechanism on 2 tasks
  (`bugfix_inventory`, `edge_case_coverage`) with two independently-worded
  weak checks for comparison; this sweep is a broader (not deeper) check -
  it confirms the *strong* evaluator's behavior alone, at every task, not a
  second weak-vs-strong disagreement per task.
- `edge_case_coverage` is included only for its unfixed-seed score, since
  it is deliberately not agent-facing (see its own `task.md`) and has no
  separate golden fix or regression-test path of its own - its
  reward-hacking behavior is already covered directly by
  `eval/reward_replication.py`.

## What this is not

This is **not** a baseline-vs-advanced agent comparison, and it says
nothing about how a coding agent actually performs on 14 of these 15
tasks - no model was involved in producing this table. See README's
"Baseline vs. advanced" section and "Limitations" for the real, N=1,
live-agent evidence that exists (one task, `bugfix_inventory`), and
CHANGELOG's "Phase 7" entry for exactly why a live multi-task agent run
could not be executed in this session.
